1. Exposure index

1.1 Facility aggregation

The TRI Basic Data File is chemical-release-form level. 77,295 rows describe 21,482 facilities; the largest single facility files 218 forms. Aggregate first:

m_f = SUM over that facility's forms of ( "5.1 - FUGITIVE AIR" + "5.2 - STACK AIR" )   [pounds, 2024]

Fugitive plus stack air is the airborne fraction. Total releases (column 107) includes water, land and underground injection, which do not reach a school through its ventilation system. Negative values, which EPA uses for data-entry corrections, are clipped to zero.

Source: src/01_build_exposure.py, function aggregate_tri. Writes data/derived/tri_facilities_2024.csv.

1.2 The index

E_s = SUM over facilities f with d_sf <= 50 km of
        m_f / max(d_sf, 0.1 km)^2

d_sf   great-circle chord distance, cKDTree on radian coordinates,
       multiplied by 6371 km. At 50 km the chord understates the arc
       by under 0.1 percent.
k      500 nearest facilities retained. The cap never binds: the median
       school has 50 TRI facilities within 50 km.
0.1 km distance floor. A school is a building, not a point, and no school
       sits inside a fenceline. Caps one facility's contribution at m_f/0.01.

1.3 Normalisation

EXPOSURE_s = min( 100 , 100 * E_s / Q99(E) )

Anchored on the 99th percentile so the published tier cutpoints (0.5 / 10 / 25 / 50 / 75) remain comparable. The scale is relative by construction: a school at 100 is in the top percentile of American schools, not above any health threshold. Because the top percentile is clipped to a single value, the percentile-band analysis in section 4 ranks on the unnormalised E_air_raw_lbs_per_km2 instead.

2. Linkage and missingness

2.1 Identifier validation

LEAID = zfill(ncesNumber, 7)
keep LEAID only if LEAID[0:2] == FIPS(stateCode), else treat as unlinkable

Without the prefix check, New Hampshire's six-digit local code 502960 pads to 0502960, a real Arkansas LEAID, merging New Hampshire's Benton onto Arkansas's Benton. Duplicate identifiers fall from 2,416 rows to 16, and the school file stops inflating from 102,178 to 102,866 rows (688 duplicate rows across 602 schools). 869 identifiers are rejected.

2.2 The outcome coding rule

HAS_VENT = 1   if isEsserAUsedFundsVentilation is True
           0   if False
           .   otherwise  (blank means the district did not answer)

Blank is missing, not zero. Across the linkable states, reading blanks as zeros moves the national rate from 57.7 to 53.9 percent; at community scale it moves Bronx County from 44.8 to 5.5 percent.

10. What is not modelled

Each district makes one funding decision covering all of its schools, so school-level observations are not independent within districts and the reported standard errors are too small. State fixed effects absorb part of this. A district-clustered specification is the obvious next robustness check and is not run here. The outcome is a binary flag, not a dollar amount: a district that spent forty thousand dollars on filters and one that spent twelve million on air handlers are recorded identically. Facilities that report to TRI are a subset of emitters, bounded by reporting thresholds and covered sectors, so a school beside a large non-reporting source scores as unexposed. The design is cross-sectional and every estimate is associational. The tail result rests on 81 schools with a Wilson interval from 33.0 to 54.1 percent, and should be read as a well-supported hypothesis rather than a settled quantity.
